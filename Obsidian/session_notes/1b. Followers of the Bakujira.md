# Followers of the Bakujira
Act 1, Session 2
Session date: loosely 13/03
Location: Dathmir By The Sea

Introduction of Gale van Gllydall, a furry gnome bard with a love for life and harder recreational substances beyond moss

Spoke with Reeve Edomin (with husband Halrin) about missing priestess and missing people.

Recently the fishing boat of Davey & Sons was found on the water, having been attacked and the crew missing. It was brought back to the docks. 

Checked the Francesca's Bounty, the boat belonging to Davey & Sons. Found attack marks resembling maardspawn, water based creatures that are aligned to a god.

Returned to tavern and enjoyed a successful strip show by Vash.

Think we spoke to the Reeve again and found a tie to the Followers of the Bakujira. The Reeve revealed the existence of smuggling caves used by the fisherman further down the coast.

Took Nefra and the boat out onto the water and found a cave. While exploring, got attacked by crab creatures that almost overwhelmed the party. Found people baked into the walls, still partly alive but in a horrific state.

Entered the deepest part of the cave and found 2 *gnomes?* talking to a half spider/crab half person queen, with the priestess chained up unconscious. The spider queen cut down the *gnomes?* and then the main fight started. During the fight we learn the spider queen intended to use the priestess to transform into another queen.
The fight became very tough, being almost overwhelmed by both the queen and smaller spider crabs. Nefra was killed during the fight, but as all looked doomed resurrected with golden-metallic skin and cut down the spider queen, ending the fight. The players opted to kill the remaining spider crabs attempting to flee, to Gylly's dismay.

The party escorted the priestess out the cave to awaiting guards/inquisition, and explained the story. Nefra was escorted away by the inquisition, while the party were offered a reward from the dragon Countess Calladria awaiting in the city of Aurixis. 

Party travel to Aurixis by convoy, but outside of the city are ambushed by goblins. Party slay them all, and continue onwards.

In the city Nefra is escorted away by inquisition guards, we identify his parents in the crowd and follow them back to their bakery, providing them with information on Nefra (after Vash and Gylly tell them their son died). The party opt to sleep in very nice looking Reflective Scribe instead of the budget Thoraxis Inn, with the city paying for our stay.

No seducing of Lion man in tavern, many party members upset
