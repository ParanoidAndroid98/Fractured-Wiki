# Root and Lantern
Act 1, Session 3
Session date: 23/05
Location: Aurixis

Wake up, Nefra in Reflective Scribe with us, he had been ordered to be let go by the dragon from the inquisition. He will join us at the palace. The party head straight to the palace which is elevated in the sky, and must be reached by magical teleportation towers.
We are introduced to the heads of houses, each head of a thing in Aurixis. Their sygils are a hammer, vines, a scroll, and *missed detail*.

Dragon arrives through opening in ceiling, then transforms into Countess Calladria, a godly 9' woman. 

Nefra is brought up first and is rewarded to become a knight of the realm.

Knights can be one of:
- Holy knight
- House knight
- Knight of the Realm

We are given the Root and Lanten as a home (deed and access), as well as 100GP each.

We go to find the inn is old and overgrown. As Vash cuts vines off the front door, he is attacked by the plant growth, and we have to cut him free from entanglement. 

We opt to go ask around about the tavern. In a hipster coffee shop we speak with a neighbor and find the previous owners were Mara and Elien, who (*died? left? I think left*)

We go back to the tavern, Gylly transforms into a boar in this moment, then turns back again causing distrust amongst the characters of whether they are safe around Gylly. Gylly later sits with the tree and has a vision about the trees origin and sees a link to the domain of order alongside other visions.
