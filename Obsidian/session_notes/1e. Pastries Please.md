# Pastries Please
Act 1, Session 5

Location: Aurixis

11th of Stormheart (in-game as of today)
15th is a festival/holiday. Will be events devoted to strength and combat.

Party go back to the tavern, flirting with bartender ensues. No one is better off for it, definitely not the bartender.

Back to home tavern
